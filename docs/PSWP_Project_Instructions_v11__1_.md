## 2. Standing rules (settled; do not relitigate mid-session)

1. **Work Order.** PK Charged = ledger Work Order whenever populated; WO Task is the header only; blanks fall back to the header and are flagged Unallocated; NA-safe (fillna('')).
2. **Per-line nature.** Line narration first, then matched creditor narration, then sighted invoice line evidence (rules 16/17). Generic narrations cannot confirm nature: Nature Basis = Vendor inference (unconfirmed), status Partial, until invoice lines are captured.
3. **Full capture.** Every column of every source export stored verbatim in the Register grey block; parsed references in their own columns; matched creditor fields in the Enquiry block; sighted invoice detail in the rule 17 green block. No invoice information is dropped, ever.
4. **Theme governance.** Theme is a live Theme_Map lookup, never hardcoded; split rows rather than overwrite; restate all years on any new taxonomy; never mix vintages across FY columns. Re-theming on sighted evidence changes Nature Category, never the Theme cell. Nature Category must exist in Theme_Map A5:A31.
5. **T9 quarantine.** Accrual reversals and recodes are T9 events; trend and share on gross operational spend (T9 excluded); never trend on net across an FY boundary; waste (T10) read net per year.
6. **Journal pairing.** Read every reversal, actual and recode as a set.
7. **Amounts.** GL ex-GST; incl-GST estimate x1.1; matched creditor incl-GST overrides the estimate; a printed total overrides both; printed GST captured as printed. TechOne incl/1.1 derivation yields 1-2c differences (artefact, rule 17 tol1c). incl/1.1 applied to a GST-FREE printed total IS an error (Hilltops pattern).
8. **Contractor identification.** Tier 1: creditor history/enquiry with ABR ABN, or sighted invoice under rule 17. Tier 2: narration-named supplier or anchored series inference (series-inferred, confirm follow-up). Tier 3: series pattern alone stays Unidentified. Never present an inference as confirmed; never carry an ABN from one line onto an inferred line. Series can be multi-vendor; same-name vendors can be different entities across FYs - the printed ABN decides.
9. **LineKey.** first8(Document Unique ID)-NaturalAccount-PKCharged-seq; uniqueness asserted every load.
10. **Control totals.** Every version ties to each source extract subtotal to the cent and returns TRUE on all checks before shipping. Enrichment builds never change the register total.
11. **Scope symmetry.** Cross-year comparison only where scope matches; asymmetries stated in Method 1.0 and Coverage.
12. **Creditor-history clearance route.** Full APLEDGER histories, one per major series, are the efficient prior-year identification route. Duplicate screening is a GATE: md5 every upload against Data_Acquisition before any parse; screen every corpus id (prefix-aware) against Evidence_Invoices column A before the match table is trusted; a re-sighting is NOT re-captured, and HELD rows must still carry no EvID after the build (v32 precedent, Kachel 7546). The same applies to journal source documents: a re-pulled Document Line Table is audited leg by leg against the embedded copy and, if identical, not re-captured (v122 precedent).
13. **Binder evidence route.** The deliverable of a binder parse is rule 16 line capture plus rule 17 register capture, never a per-invoice summary. Reconcile before reporting extraction losses; last-page totals are a capture note, not a loss; dedupe duplicate copy pages by normalised hash; normalise PK typos on join and record the printed form.
14. **Self-containment.** The workbook stands alone: source exports verbatim, creditor lines, SE2, invoice evidence, these instructions and the acquisition register embedded as sheets. Anything a knowledge file states about structure is also stated in Method.
15. **Discard-and-re-pull.** Originals are not kept once embedded.
16. **Comprehensive invoice line capture.** Every sighted invoice stored at line-item granularity, verbatim, before its source is discarded: Evidence_Invoices (one row per invoice), Evidence_Invoice_Lines (one row per printed line incl. zero-amount rows). (a) No paraphrase; (b) captured lines reconcile to the printed subtotal to the cent with a live check per invoice (incl-GST templates reconcile to the printed Total Inc GST, basis recorded); (c) Nature Basis "Sighted invoice line"; (d) correspondence in binders is evidence too. A summary row in place of line capture is a critical failure.
17. **Register-line capture, and what Confirmed means.** Confirmed = invoice read, every printed detail on the register line's green block (cols CJ:DW), Nature Category/Detail restated from that row's own sighted lines (Amendment 1: empty or generic Nature Detail is Partial, and build scripts refuse the upgrade), and three live checks TRUE: (1) captured lines = printed subtotal; (2) register amount = printed subtotal; (3) printed GST = 10% of printed subtotal. Ratified variants, each returning the literal "TRUE" and tagged [check variant] in the anomalies note (a control counts the tags): check 1 incl-GST basis; check 2 one-cent tolerance; check 2 derivation equality (incl/1.1); check 2 split-posting (each register line ties its own captured lines by LineKey); check 2 SUM-TIE (v23: one printed line posted as several non-zero AP rows with no printed counterpart - each sibling's check 2 sums the sibling register amounts against its printed target); check 2 PARTIAL-SCOPE (v32: a Council-wide invoice where the loaded PS/WP scope carries a subset of the printed lines - check 2 ties the register amount to that row's own line(s) by LineKey; checks 1 and 3 tie the whole printed invoice; the association must be FIXED by evidence, else the row is HELD, not green-blocked); check 3 GST-free; check 3 rounding tolerance (1c, 2c per-line baskets). Tolerances always ROUND(ABS(...),2)<=; Excel ROUND is half-away-from-zero, so precompute with Decimal ROUND_HALF_UP. Blanks recorded as "(blank as printed)"; fields the layout does not carry "(not printed)"; never filled from another source. Journals rest on pairing, utilities on the system record. Status and Verdict stay independent. Zero-amount companion AP lines are never green-blocked. Matching aids (TechOne reference, creditor code, PK, amount, Document Unique ID) never populate capture fields (19.9). Vendor boilerplate (payment/terms) is stored once on Vendor_Boilerplate and cited by BP: key from the green block (Amendment 2; a control proves every cited key exists).
18. **One-script build.** All edits in one build script run once: one save, one convert-route recalc, one verify pass. Content held as data (corpus JSON, brief JSON), never ad-hoc writes. Workbook reads targeted. A verify failure fixed by a targeted patch is applied to the script of record and the file, then one further recalc-and-verify cycle; the patched script ships.
19. **Session efficiency.**
    - 19.1 Knowledge files as listed above; a session that changes structure, adds a parser or finds a trap updates the affected file in the same session and ships it.
    - 19.2 Structured input first: corpus JSON per extraction prompt v4, repaired and gated (pswp_json_repair.py, families F1-F13) before any use; a corpus declaring gate RED or AMBER is never built from, and pathologies P1-P7 are parse failures, not tie failures; repairs restate from retained page text, never guess; a green gate proves amounts, not wording - spot-check verbatim fidelity (five-word shingles) on every captured invoice where page text is retained, one per vendor otherwise; extraction tool identity logged in Data_Acquisition. A corpus that fails ANY of its own gates is logged, held and NOT part-built from (v122 precedent, corpus_mix1).
    - 19.3 Phase split: batches over ~50 invoices run as a parse session (corpus JSON to outputs) and a build session.
    - 19.4 Failure-only inspection (~300 chars around a failure, never full pages).
    - 19.5 Counts-only console; work files to outputs.
    - 19.6 Toolkit reads positions from Config, never hardcodes.
    - **19.7 Atomic build phase (AMENDED v11).** build -> recalc (convert route, isolated LibreOffice profile with OOXMLRecalcMode=0; recalc.py banned) -> verify (exact "TRUE" on every EI control, every sighted line's three checks, every recon row, both citation limbs, Coverage all-years tie, register total) -> ship, as ONE SCRIPT that runs the whole chain unattended. The script is launched detached and polled to completion; the session ships only on the script's own clean-verify exit. No stage may be run by hand, no stage may be re-ordered, and a partial run is DISCARDED and re-run from the top rather than resumed. Match table re-verified 100% when the register version moved since the parse, else a 5-row sample; no interim status reports; context discipline (schema-level summaries only).
    - 19.8 Reads via python-calamine, writes via openpyxl (numeric strings from calamine carry ".0" - strip). A second openpyxl open of the workbook to check something costs ~52 seconds against ~4 for calamine; never do it.
    - 19.9 Matching aids are not evidence.
    - 19.10 Citation drift: both citation limbs (EI col 13 recon-row and register-row; Register col 28 recon-row) are rewritten from the EvID maps on every recon relocation, never by positional shift or blanket regex, and the verify pass audits both.
    - 19.11 Tail shift: totals-row SUMs rewritten explicitly (self-inclusive SUM trap); panels citing absolute criteria rows are repointed on any move. A block appended above a controls region has its controls and summary rows REWRITTEN explicitly, never shifted. A controls region MOVED to another sheet has every panel citing its absolute rows rewritten explicitly in the same build (v123 precedent: Evidence_Invoices!C3788 counted TRUEs over Evidence_Invoice_Lines!$E$8956:$E$12717 and was repointed to EIL_Controls!$E$5:$E$3766; a build that moves a block without this sweep ships a silent FALSE).
20. **Brief-driven build.** Batch builds run through pswp_build_batch.py: positions from Config; batch facts (vendors, EvID prefixes, BP keys, categories, variants, partial-scope selectors, held rows, Open Items, Method/Handover/Data_Acquisition text, verify pins) from a batch brief JSON. A session authors the brief, not a script; new mechanics that the driver cannot express are added to the driver (shipped, rule 19.1) rather than written ad hoc. `--dry-run` runs every pre-write gate; the default runs the full 19.7 chain and ships only on a clean verify.
21. **Pull lists are keyed to the document FILE (NEW v11).** One TechOne Document Line Table export covers one document file, and a document file can carry many journal references. Pull queues are ranked by ABSOLUTE NET movement into the assessed scope, never gross, and tiered by evidence route: pull only where no attachment exists in TechOne; sight the attachment where one does; never pull an RJ reversing document, which nets to exactly zero and is read as a pair under rules 5 and 6.

## 3. Classification framework

Tiers 1/2/3 and Nature Basis values as in rule 8. Status: Confirmed | Partial | Pending evidence (Confirmed per rule 17 only). Verdicts: Correct | Review | Confirm | Journal (net-zero) | Journal (reversal); never Confirmed-Correct on vendor inference alone. Themes v2 T1-T13 per Theme_Map. Anchored identities: Vendor_Series sheet (authoritative), not this file.

## 4. Pipeline

Per ledger export: verify criteria; calamine parse; NA-safe PK/LineKeys; contractor by history/enquiry then narration then series; nature engine; enrichment; full-capture block; Theme_Map; recalc; verify; spot-check.

Per invoice batch: md5 duplicate check; corpus JSON or raw-text fallback with page text retained; repair and gate; reconcile every invoice (rule 16); EvID duplicate screen; match by Reference (AP lines only, truncation-aware); author the brief; rule 20 build; discard source only after ship.

Per journal batch: md5 duplicate check; map every Document Line Table to its document file and journal reference(s) via Register col 69; screen against Journal_Sources; audit any re-sighting leg by leg and do not re-capture; embed new documents verbatim with leg-level in-scope flags; prove each document nets to zero and each in-scope leg sum ties its own register lines; refresh Journal_Pull.

## 5. Workbook specification

PS_WP_Transaction_Register_{scope}_v{N}[_HANDOVER].xlsx, Cambria, money $#,##0.00;($#,##0.00);"-", dates D-Mon-YYYY, live formulas only (SUMIFS/COUNTIF/INDEX/MATCH; never XLOOKUP/FILTER/SORT). Sheet list, column maps, check formulas, positions: Config (positions), Method (structure), PSWP_Register_Schema.md (working aid). Register cols 1-127 with the green block at 88-127 (CJ:DW).

STRUCK v11: the former "pending ratification: freeze settled formulas on Confirmed prior-FY rows" is withdrawn. It was tested end to end at v122 and rejected: freezing eleven columns on all 6,565 Confirmed prior-FY rows removes 31,289 of 214,258 formulas and saves 5.5 seconds of a 175-second chain. Permanently disabling self-testing on 6,565 rows is not worth 3%. Freeze_Log is retained as the precedent record only. No formula on a Confirmed row is ever converted to a value.
